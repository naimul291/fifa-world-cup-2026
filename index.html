Any Questions?

How will I receive my match tickets?

After your purchase is confirmed, your tickets will be delivered digitally to your registered email address and will also be available in your account dashboard before the match.

Can I cancel or refund my ticket?

Ticket cancellations and refunds are subject to FIFA's official ticketing policy. Please review the cancellation terms before completing your purchase.

Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?

Yes. Eligible tickets can be transferred to another person through the official FIFA ticket management system, subject to FIFA's transfer rules.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, as well as selected digital payment methods. All transactions are protected with secure SSL encryption.

Do I need a Fan ID or passport to enter the stadium?

Depending on the host country's entry requirements and FIFA regulations, you may need a valid passport and additional travel or identification documents. Please check the latest official requirements before your trip.
